Privacy policy

This privacy policy explains the Google account data involved in a personal OAuth client configured for the Danex360 Google Assistant plugin for Elgato Stream Deck. It is intended for the account owner's private, noncommercial use.

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Data accessed

With the account owner's consent, the locally installed plugin may use the Google Assistant SDK and the account email address. This allows it to identify the connected account, submit requested Assistant commands or queries, and receive responses. The developer identifies the following OAuth scopes: assistant-sdk-prototype and userinfo.email. The Google consent screen lists the actual permissions requested at authorization time.

2. How the data is used

Google data is used to authenticate the user's account and carry out requests initiated through Stream Deck buttons, including queries and permitted smart-home actions. The private OAuth project owner does not independently collect, analyze, sell, or monetize Google account data.

3. Storage and security

This website is a static informational site. It does not receive Google OAuth tokens, account credentials, Assistant requests, or responses, and it does not use forms or analytics scripts. According to Danex360's published privacy policy, the plugin stores encrypted OAuth tokens locally within Stream Deck's application data directory; the plugin is maintained by Danex360, not by the owner of this OAuth client.

4. Third parties and sharing

The plugin communicates with Google services to carry out the account owner's requests. The plugin developer states that it does not send credentials, voice recordings, queries, or other personal data to servers controlled by the developer. Google's own policies apply to data processed by Google. This site's hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process ordinary website access information under its own privacy policy.

5. Choices and revocation

The account owner may stop using the integration, remove the plugin's account connection, or revoke access at Google Account third-party connections. For locally stored data, consult the plugin's settings and the developer's instructions.

6. Changes and contact

This document will be updated if the private OAuth configuration or its data practices change. Questions about this OAuth configuration may be directed to the user-support email displayed on its Google OAuth consent screen. For questions about the underlying plugin's data-handling behavior, consult Danex360's privacy policy.

Independent project notice.

This policy describes a personal OAuth configuration. It does not supersede the third-party plugin developer's privacy policy or Google's policies.

Personal-use OAuth configuration. Not an official Google, Elgato, or Danex360 product.
